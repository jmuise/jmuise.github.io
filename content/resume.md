## Experience

career ladder" and more "learn whatever the problem in front of me demanded."
That turned out to be a strength: legacy enterprise systems, full-stack web
apps, cloud-native microservices, and now AI/ML. Lately that instinct is
pointed at developer experience -- bringing the same deterministic, testable
rigor I've always brought to CI/CD to the newer, messier world of agentic AI
tooling.{{< resume-pdf-note >}}

{{< eyebrow >}}Foundations{{< /eyebrow >}}

This was the last of several co-op placements during my degree -- the one that
turned into a full-time offer. Started as one of two developers building a web

the company's older world of COBOL, Fortran, and Visual Basic. As much a

{{< eyebrow >}}Range & Scale{{< /eyebrow >}}

Joined a larger team building custom clothes-sizing software for a company

deployments for greater scalability, and cutting a key transaction path from
900ms to 300ms through targeted performance testing. This is where

{{< eyebrow >}}Broadening the Toolkit{{< /eyebrow >}}

{{< eyebrow >}}Healthcare & Cloud{{< /eyebrow >}}

{{< eyebrow >}}Going Independent{{< /eyebrow >}}
